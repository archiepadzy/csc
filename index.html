Our Heritage & Future

The Backbone of the Zimbabwean Livestock Industry.

The Cold Storage Company (CSC) Limited stands as a cornerstone of national agriculture. With our headquarters in Bulawayo, we have spent decades perfecting the art of meat processing and cold-chain management.

Today, we are undergoing a massive technological revitalization. By combining our historic expertise with world-class HACCP-certified facilities and humane slaughter protocols, we provide an unbroken cold chain that ensures "Simply the Best Beef Worldwide" reaches local and global markets safely.
